### The Mission
This site operates as a digital garden and research laboratory exploring the friction between ancient human teleology and modern algorithmic systems.

I am mapping the transition from the **Industrial Stream** of the internet (ephemeral, extractive) to an **Edenic** information architecture (spatial, formative). My goal is to design systems that preserve human agency, spiritual formation, and biological time.

**Current Research Vectors:**
* *[[Holy HCI]]* — Interface design for the soul.
* *[[Digital Discernment]]* — Cognitive filtering in an age of noise.
* *[[Techno-Pastoralism]]* — Returning to organic rhythms in digital spaces.
