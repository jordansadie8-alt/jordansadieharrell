## Definition
A theological term (specifically from the Eastern Orthodox distinction between *Ousia* and *Energeia*) referring to the active, perceptible power of God interacting with creation. Unlike God's *Essence* (which is unknowable and transcendent), His *Energies* are the means by which humans experience, participate in, and are sustained by the Divine life.

**Divine Energy** is the answer to the question: "How can I touch God if God is totally other?"
* **The Sun Metaphor:** The Church Fathers compare God to the Sun. You cannot touch the *Essence* of the Sun (the ball of burning gas)—it would vaporize you. But you *can* touch and be warmed by the *Energies* of the Sun (the rays of light and heat). The rays *are* the Sun, but they are the Sun *in action toward you*.
* **The Practical Application:** For the "Strong Woman," Divine Energy is the fuel for **[[Indefatigable Agency]]**. When we say "Grace," we don't just mean "forgiveness"; we mean a literal injection of spiritual horsepower. It is the ontological "current" that runs through the "wire" of the human soul.
* **The Distinction:** This protects against Pantheism (I am not God) and Deism (God is not absent). I participate in God's *energy*, just as iron in a fire participates in the fire's heat without becoming the fire itself.

## Signs / markers
-   **Vitality beyond Biology:** The ability to love or work beyond what one's sleep schedule or calorie intake should allow (e.g., the "Strength of the Martyrs").
-   **Luminosity:** A sense of "lightness" or clarity in the mind, opposed to the "heaviness" of the flesh.
-   **Warmth:** A literal or metaphorical sensation of heat in the chest (the "burning heart" of the disciples at Emmaus).

## Examples 
-   **"The Vibe":** In secular terms, we feel when someone has "good energy." Theologically, this is a dim reflection of the reality that souls broadcast what they contain.
-   **Nuclear Power:** A physical metaphor. A small amount of matter (the Eucharist/the Soul) releases massive energy when the atom (the Ego) is split/opened.
-   **Resilience:** The single mother working three jobs who somehow maintains a sense of joy. She is tapping into a grid that the "rich and bored" cannot see.

## Archetypal Figures & Case Studies

> [!abstract] The Burning Bush (Moses)
> **The Trait:** *Sustained Intensity.*
> **The Analysis:** The bush burns with fire (Divine Energy) but is not consumed. This is the model for the Saint. If you run on human energy (wood), you burn up (Burnout). If you run on Divine Energy (fire), you burn without being consumed. You become a vehicle for the energy.
> **Lesson:** The goal is not to be the fuel, but to be the wick.

> [!abstract] St. Gregory Palamas
> **The Trait:** *The Defender of the Light.*
> **The Analysis:** He fought the rationalists who claimed that God could only be known intellectually. He argued that the monks on Mount Athos were literally seeing **Uncreated Light** (Divine Energy). He established that we can *participate* in God, not just think about Him.
> **Lesson:** God is an experience, not just a syllogism.

> [!abstract] The Woman with the Issue of Blood
> **The Trait:** *The Tactile Transfer.*
> **The Analysis:** She touches the hem of Christ’s garment. Jesus asks, "Who touched me?" because He felt *"power"* (virtue/energy) go out from Him. This is Divine Energy as a transferable force, accessible through the "touch" of faith.
> **Lesson:** The Energy is always broadcasting; Faith adjusts the receiver.

## The Narrative Arc (The Charge)

### 1. The Disconnection (The Dead Zone)
The person is operating on "Biological Energy" (ATP/Glucose/Adrenaline). They are fluctuating between high anxiety and exhaustion. They are "Dark Matter."

### 2. The Contact (The Grounding)
Through prayer, sacrament, or **Kenosis**, the person touches the source. They stop trying to generate energy and start trying to *receive* it.

### 3. The Transfiguration (The Glow)
The energy enters the system. It clarifies the mind (**Nepsis**) and strengthens the will (**Synergy**). The person becomes "[[Incandescent]]".

### 4. The Radiation (The Overflow)
The energy spills out. Others feel "better" or "calmer" just by being near this person. This is the definition of "Holiness"—being a radioactive carrier of Divine Energy.

## Contrasts
-   **Opposite:** **[[Entropy]]:** The natural tendency of closed systems to run down into disorder. Divine Energy reverses entropy.
-   **Opposite:** **Created Grace:** The Western scholastic idea that grace is a created "commodity" God gives us. The Eastern view says Grace *is* God Himself (His Energy).
-   **Related-but-not-the-same:** **Adrenaline:** Biological stress energy. It feels jagged and leaves you crashed. Divine Energy feels still and leaves you stable.
