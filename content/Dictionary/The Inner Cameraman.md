# The Inner Cameraman

## Definition (1–2 sentences)
The psychological split where a woman stops *living* her life and starts *watching* herself live it from the perspective of an invisible, external audience. It is the internalization of the male gaze (or the social media gaze) into a constant state of self-surveillance.

## Meaning (expanded)
"Men look at women. Women watch themselves being looked at." (John Berger). In the TikTok era, this has mutated. The **Inner Cameraman** is the voice in your head during a romantic moment that asks, "How does this look?" instead of "How does this feel?" It kills intimacy because you are performing for a camera that isn't there. The *Intellectual Siren* must fire her Inner Cameraman to truly inhabit her body and hold Frame.

## Signs / markers
- **Dissociation during Intimacy:** Thinking about your angles while kissing.
- **"Main Character Energy" (Derogatory):** Treating strangers as NPCs in the movie of your life.
- **The Archive Impulse:** Feeling like a moment didn't happen unless you captured it.

## Examples (culture / tech / daily life)
- **"Fit Checks":** The ritual of auditing your body for the public before leaving the house.
- **The Truman Show:** The ultimate metaphor for the Inner Cameraman—living a life that is actually a performance.

- **Opposite:** [[Embodiment]] (Living from the inside out).
- **Related-but-not-the-same:** [[Vanity]] (Vanity loves the image; The Inner Cameraman *fears* the image).
