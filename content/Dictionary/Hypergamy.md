## Definition
The instinctive, cross-cultural tendency for women to seek mates of equal or higher socio-economic status, competence, or dominance hierarchy placement. Biologically, it serves as a selection filter designed to ensure security and resource provision for offspring.

Often colloquially associated with financial acquisition ("gold digging"), Hypergamy is fundamentally a psychological filter for **competence**. It is the subconscious drive to evaluate whether a potential mate is capable of navigating the world effectively and providing leadership.

In the modern era, where women possess independent financial resources, Hypergamy often shifts from seeking financial dependency to seeking **intellectual and spiritual competence**. This shift suggests that high-performing women may narrow their mating pool not due to arbitrary standards, but because their competence baseline is higher, reducing the number of partners who occupy a relatively higher position in the competence hierarchy.

## Signs & Markers
* **"The Ick":** A visceral repulsion felt when a potential partner displays submissive, indecisive, or socially incompetent behavior. Evolutionary psychology posits this as a biological alarm system signaling "low status" or a lack of safety.
* **Fitness Testing:** Subconscious behavioral tests ("Shit-testing") administered to check for emotional stability, frame strength, and social dominance.
* **Respect-First Attraction:** The psychological phenomenon where sexual attraction is contingent upon first establishing respect for a partner's intellect or capability.

## Examples (Sociological & Cultural)
* **The CEO Dilemma:** Successful women who occupy dominant roles professionally often express a preference for partners who are *more* dominant or competent than themselves in the domestic sphere, allowing for a shift in polarity.
* **Celebrity Assortative Mating:** A trend where female public figures rarely date "civilians" or fans, whereas male public figures frequently do. This illustrates the tendency to look "up" or "across" the hierarchy rather than down.
* **"High Value Man" Discourse:** Internet subcultures often reduce the hypergamous instinct to material metrics (e.g., the "6-6-6" rule: 6 figures, 6 feet, 6 pack), though the biological driver is rooted more deeply in survival and competence.

## Contrasts
* **Opposite:** Hypogamy — The practice of dating "down" in status (statistically rarer for women, more common for men).
* **Distinction:** Gold Digging — A transactional or predatory pursuit of wealth. Hypergamy is distinct as an evolutionary survival instinct regarding overall competence and genetic fitness, rather than purely financial extraction.
