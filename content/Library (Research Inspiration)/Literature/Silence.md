# Silence

## Why I saved this
- **The "Broken Interface":** To analyze what happens when the "User Interface" with God (prayer) goes silent. What is faith when there is no feedback?
- **Cultural Framing:** The clash between the "Western" interface of Christianity (logical, categorical) and the "Eastern" swamp of Japan (amorphous, absorbing).
- **The Teleology of Failure:** Rodrigues fails as a priest and a hero. The book asks: Is there a teleology in apostasy?

## Key themes
- **The Silence of God:** The central trauma—why does God not intervene in suffering?
- **Judas as Hero:** A provocative look at Kichijiro, the weak betrayer, asking if he is more beloved by Christ than the strong martyrs.
- **The Mudswamp:** Japan as a spiritual "swamp" where foreign ideas rot and change form.

## Key quotes
- "Lord, I resented your silence." / "I was not silent. I suffered beside you."
- "Sin is not only stealing or lying... Sin is for one man to walk over the life of another and to remain indifferent."
- "Trample! It was to be trampled on by men that I was born into this world."

## Notes / takeaways
- **Framing Connection:** Endō frames the "Fumie" (stepping on the icon) not as a rejection of Christ, but as an *act* of Christ (sacrificing one's pride to save others). This is a **re-framing** of the highest order.
- **Psychological Insight:** The psychology of **Cognitive Dissonance** in the believer.
- **Sociology of Persecution:** How a state system systematically dismantles an ideology not by killing, but by inducing shame.
