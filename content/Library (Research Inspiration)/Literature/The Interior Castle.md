# The Interior Castle

## Why I saved this
- **The "Architecture of the Soul":** The ultimate "System Design" for the human spirit. She maps the soul as a castle with seven mansions (interfaces) leading to the center.
- **Psychological Precision:** Teresa analyzes self-deception, hallucinations, and ego-traps with the precision of a modern psychologist.
- **The "Alpha" Mystic:** Teresa was a reformer, a founder, and a powerhouse. She embodies the "Strong Woman" who commands men and talks back to God.

## Key themes
- **Self-Knowledge:** You cannot know God if you do not know yourself. The first mansion is self-awareness.
- **The Prayer of Quiet:** The transition from "Active Interface" (talking to God) to "Passive Interface" (being held by God).
- **Spiritual Marriage:** The final union of will.

## Key quotes
- "It is foolish to think that we will enter heaven without entering into ourselves."
- "Humility is truth."
- "Let nothing disturb you, let nothing frighten you... God alone suffices."

## Notes / takeaways
- **Framing Connection:** The "Diamond Castle" metaphor. She frames the soul as a vast, beautiful space, countering the "worm/sinner" narrative.
- **Psychological Insight:** Her distinction between "melancholy" (depression) and "dark night" (spiritual growth) is clinically relevant.
- **Sociology of Gender:** She wrote this under the scrutiny of the Inquisition, using "I'm just a silly woman" as a rhetorical shield to teach profound theology.
